A nonparametric regression that fits a fresh weighted line around every point of interest. Built four kernels (tricubic, Epanechnikov, quartic, exponential), derived the weighted normal equations, and validated the estimator against a known signal with a train–test split.

Predicting education level from demographic data

48,219 individual records from the 2013 Liberia Demographic and Health Survey. I compared logistic regression, k-NN, decision trees, and random forests on the same train–test split, tuning each rather than accepting defaults. Random forest won at 72.2% accuracy. Wealth was the strongest single correlate of education (r = 0.26); household size was effectively uncorrelated.

In practice

How I've implemented my Data Science expertise in practice.

Systems, quickly

Control testing means landing in an unfamiliar system every few weeks — AWS, Snowflake, OneLake, Elastic, ServiceNow — and learning its data model well enough to know what evidence actually proves a control works. The systems change; the reasoning doesn't.

Leveraging AI

Built a custom Gemini assistant that reads control evidence and classifies it as sufficient or insufficient against encryption policy requirements — turning a slow, subjective review into a consistent, repeatable pass.

SQL at scale

Pulling, joining, and validating data from client systems in SQL and Python — including the unglamorous part, writing checks that catch quality problems before they reach a deliverable.

Reporting people use

Dashboards and success metrics for internal HR reporting, built so the people running the process could answer their own questions without asking an analyst.
