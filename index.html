Clicks through "load more" until the catalogue is exhausted, then parses each partner card into eight fields — specializations, expertises, products, locations — where the markup varies by which sections a partner happens to have. 10,824 records out, structured and ready for analysis.

Locally weighted regression from scratch

A nonparametric regression that fits a fresh weighted line around every point of interest. Built four kernels (tricubic, Epanechnikov, quartic, exponential), derived the weighted normal equations, and validated the estimator against a known signal with a train–test split.

Multivariate locally weighted regression

Project 1, generalized: LOWESS extended to multiple features and to prediction on unseen data. Euclidean distance across the feature space, an adaptive bandwidth set by each point's k-th nearest neighbour, ridge regularization to keep the local system solvable, and ND interpolation with a nearest-neighbour fallback outside the convex hull. Cross-validated MSE of 16.8 on cars (MPG) and 143.9 on concrete (compressive strength).

  • NumPy
  • SciPy
  • k-fold CV
  • Regularization

Predicting education level from demographic data

48,219 individual records from the 2013 Liberia Demographic and Health Survey. I compared logistic regression, k-NN, decision trees, and random forests on the same train–test split, tuning each rather than accepting defaults. Random forest won at 72.2% accuracy. Wealth was the strongest single correlate of education (r = 0.26); household size was effectively uncorrelated.
